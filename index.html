The North Coast — Mackay Country

Driving the far northern edge of Scotland — and for us, this is the big one: the far northwest is Dùthaich MhicAoidh, "the Province of the Mackays," our clan's ancestral homeland. We'll visit Balnakeil Church (built by the Mackay chief in 1619), Castle Varrich (the ancient Mackay seat above the Kyle of Tongue), and the Clan Mackay Centre at Strathnaver — then drive down Strathnaver itself, the valley the family comes from. Bettyhill, where we're staying, was founded for Mackay families cleared from that same valley.

Cell coverage up here is patchy; the hotel landline is the reliable option.
